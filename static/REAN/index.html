How it works

  1. Read the terrain

    Spot risky stairs, steps and slippery ground. Risk estimates help it avoid terrain likely to cause a fall.

  2. Predict the energy

    A learned predictor checks routes against the remaining charge. It compares the energy needed for different routes before choosing.

  3. Choose a safer route

    Pick a route it can finish. It balances terrain risk and energy instead of taking the shortest way.
