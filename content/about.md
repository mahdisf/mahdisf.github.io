I'm a technical product manager for AI and engineering software. I work out how engineers actually do their jobs and decide what an AI product should do for them. Then I help a cross-functional team build it and show that it works.

I came to product management from engineering. For several years I built robotics and control systems: ROS2 software for rehabilitation exoskeletons, reinforcement-learning research for legged robots, and embedded controllers. On most of those projects I also ended up writing the requirements, coordinating the team and writing up the evidence. Since March 2026 that has been my full-time role.

When the user is a control engineer, a roboticist or a developer, that background lets me follow their workflow, question an architecture and judge whether a result is real.

<ol class="arc" role="list">
  <li>I started in engineering: robotics software, real-time control and simulation, in university labs and on freelance projects.</li>
  <li>I moved into product through ViGenX, my own project, and was promoted to Technical Product Manager in March 2026.</li>
  <li>Now I work on AI products that make complex engineering workflows faster and easier to trust.</li>
</ol>
